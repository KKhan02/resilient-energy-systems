# SEEDExchange: geographically informed conditional pandapower screen (v1)

**Status:** Source coordinates and provisional house assignments incorporated. **No AC power-flow executions included in this package.** The map is a hypothetical LV routing layer, not a verified EPM network. Use separate outputs from the earlier 324.5 m representative network.

## Exactly what is grounded

- Six specific existing EPM transformer number/node pairs, plus the separately supplied northern 25 kVA transformer with unconfirmed asset number.
- Seven precise transformer locations from user-shared EPM records, including **98380 (node 241054) at 6.237093826, -75.012599551**.
- Thirty-five user-supplied geolocated property points, each with a *candidate* transformer association.
- EPM records report 5 or 3 kVA ratings (and 25 kVA for the northern alternative), 7.621 kV nominal L-N primary, 240/120 V secondary and separate circuits 221-12/221-13. These do not prove actual LV connections.
- The base inventory is 94022:10, 106039:12, 92395:4, 85398:4, 113699:4, 98380:1, northern transformer:0. **This is a modelling assignment, not a utility service list.**

## What is NOT grounded

- Actual MV/LV wire runs, utility pole coordinates, conductor cross sections, service connections, real individual meter profiles, phase-leg connections and current protection arrangement.
- The root-side MV network is an **idealized voltage source**. MV cable impedance and upstream voltage deviations are not included.
- All LV links are generated by one of two *hypothetical* algorithms: `mst` (shortest incremental radial tree) or `star` (one independent link to the transformer per property). Straight-line link lengths are multiplied by a transparent factor such as 1.0 or 1.3 for route-detour sensitivity. These are not roads, cable paths or surveyed feeder lengths. A 1.3 multiplier increases **electrical length**, while the GeoJSON shows the straight endpoint-to-endpoint segment.
- LV loop cable: R = 1.312 ohm/km, X = 0.160 ohm/km, Imax = 150 A, borrowed as proxies from the previous v5 representative model, **not independently checked for individual assets**.
- Transformer vk = 3%, vkr = 1.8%, 2.5% no-load current; iron loss scales proportionally with rated kVA. These are engineering approximations, not utility nameplate measurements.
- 120/240 V single-phase 3-wire secondary with individual leg imbalance is NOT solved. A 3-phase-equivalent balanced model rescales nominal line voltage by sqrt(3) and powers by 3. This inherits limitations from the v5 notebook and does not validate neutral currents.
- One combined unity-PF PV/BESS inverter, at head, at half of the longest LV path (middle), or at the furthest service bus (end). It **does not simulate PV and battery as physically separate converters at distinct houses**.

Extract ZIP into repository root (so the folder becomes `case1/studies/geographic_pandapower_v1/`). Open **Miniforge Prompt** in this folder and use the existing environment containing `pandapower`, `numpy`, `pandas`, and `matplotlib`.

## Input schedule logic

- Gross-demand baseline: Use the previously produced exact household-count synthetic PyPSA demand trajectory for N >=5 if present. For the 4- and 1-house territories, scale the 5-house synthetic demand curve by N/5. **This is a declared proxy**, not measured consumption or a design optimization.
- Optimized PV+BESS: Only run when `N_homes_hourly_dispatch.csv` exists for that exact number of houses, using the selected best-economic design from the earlier MILP study. Missing cases are **skipped and itemized** in `skipped_optimized_cases.csv`. They are never treated as feasible or assigned an invented PV/BESS system.
- For associations changing group size (such as H10 moved to 106039, which becomes 13 houses), an exact newly generated generic PyPSA 13-house dispatch may be used if present. This remains hypothetical demand, not an actual meter record.
- `equal` spreads aggregate load across actual geolocated house nodes; optional `far_end_heavy` assigns 60% of demand to the farthest 25% of properties by candidate cable-path distance.
- Load PF 0.98/0.90, unity-PF inverter, no autonomous reactive control. Grid exchange is recalculated by pandapower with losses and reactive effects, rather than forced to the PyPSA connection proxy.
- The primary screening voltage is 0.97 to 1.03 pu, plus 0.95 and 1.05 reference thresholds. Transformer/line max loading 100%; conditions are only proxy electrical screens.
- The 25 kVA northern transformer is modeled when alternate households are assigned; its asset ID and service topology are not confirmed.

## Limitations and next step

A solve can converge yet show an unrealistic huge voltage drop because the candidate LV routes are long. **Do not repair a failed voltage screen by quietly inventing shorter service cables.** Report and compare alternative cable layouts and source assumptions, then use any official EPM LV circuit/pole geometry to replace candidate links if available.

The possible H14/H15 and H01/H02 associations need particular attention. Even favorable power-flow results do not prove who is physically connected to a transformer.

Resilience/islanding requires a different control mode and protection assessment. This grid-connected AC screen does not simulate outages, black start, critical load ranking, or grid-forming capability.
