# Candidate Site A — equipment footprint reference and spatial limitations

The original quoted reference package is **12 × 435 W panels = 5.22 kWp**, **four 5.12 kWh modules = 20.48 kWh nominal storage**, and a quoted 5 kW hybrid inverter, switching, mounting and accessories. The researcher's **~50 m²** figure is a provisional complete-package footprint and **NOT a dimensioned construction layout**.

### Panel surface versus installed ground area

The referenced LONGi LR5-54HTH module-family datasheet gives indicative physical dimensions 1.722 m × 1.134 m, i.e. **~1.953 m² panel surface**. Total *panel face* only is ~23.4 m² for 12 panels, 46.9 m² for 24, 78.1 m² for 40, and 156.2 m² for 80. These are **not** ground-mount footprints. Array spacing depends on tilt, height, row orientation, design shading hours, cleaning and access setbacks. A 12 × 12 m = 144 m² square was previously used only as a **hypothetical envelope test inside the polygon**; it is neither a requirement nor a verified clear buildable plot.

The digitized Site A clearing has **~1,759.26 m² gross geodesic area** and surrounds a visible building/vegetation. Sensitivities of 25%, 50%, 75% usable area are not measured. Actual contiguous space, sun access, setbacks, foundations, drainage, battery enclosure and fire separation remain unknown.

### Hardware compatibility hold point

The 5 kW Deye hybrid inverter described in the old quote appears to be a nominal **three-phase model**, while the modelled legacy LV supply uses a **120/240 V split-phase-type reference**. Verify exact SKU and actual EPM supply before proposing switchgear, topology, PV strings or storage architecture. Supplier quotations are not approvals.
