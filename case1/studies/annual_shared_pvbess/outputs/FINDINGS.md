## How to read the outputs

1. Under **this myopic local self-consumption algorithm**, C is slightly cheaper and uses less imported electricity than K at matched total equipment. This is **not a claim that C is electrically superior**. The separate pandapower snapshot study finds that two shared injection locations can improve evening voltage but increase midday voltage rise.
2. The 12-house candidate territory behind 923494 has **105 hours above the 4.5kW proxy** in the example, and the portal provided 5 **kW**, not an independently verified transformer 5kVA. Do not treat this as certified transformer overload or customer connection.
3. Battery dispatch is time-causal with SOC bounds but **does not optimize costs or headroom over future hours**. At the year boundary SOC may change. No inter-cluster private cable or real meter settlement was established.
4. The 25-year figures use **historical HOMER component quotes** and **unsourced BOS sensitivities** (0-30% hardware markup and USD 0-1500/site). Cable additions, actual installation, protection, permits, tariffs and reinforcement remain **unpriced**. This is not a complete verified lifecycle cost.
5. The 386 rows in annual_scenarios.csv include PV-only, BESS, BESS-power and export-limit variations. All explicitly say LV voltage status **NOT_SIMULATED**, as full-year AC network validation and actual phase/neutral modelling remain separate.
