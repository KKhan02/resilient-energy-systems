# Research history, old visualizations and transfer packages

This directory separates superseded figures, historical notebooks and duplicated upload ZIP files from the professor-facing figure sequence. A move is **not** a deletion from git history, and binary ZIPs remain in historical commits. Authoritative source code, raw model outputs, validation manifests and test suites remain under `case1/studies/`, `case1/data/` and `case1/legacy/`.

Use [the numbered professor walkthrough](../professor_walkthrough/README.md) for the narrative and [research status](../RESEARCH_STATUS_AND_AUDIT.md) for review limitations.

The [relocation index](RELOCATION_INDEX.csv) records **736 original-to-archive file paths** from this cleanup. Most legacy generated figures moved here without altering their source results or tests. The former root-level `SEEDExchange_*` review packages are preserved under `past_review_packages/`; prior v3 figures are under `legacy_v3_figures/`; 480 original full-study all-case PNG/SVG images are under `legacy_joint_optimization_figures/`; original hackathon notebooks are under `original_hackathon_notebooks/`; old output ZIPs are under `transfer_zips/`.

The archived legacy linked Pareto/cost plot families predate the unconnected technology-sizing plots chosen for the walkthrough. The root miscellaneous upload artifacts and old transfer ZIPs are retained here for traceability. Do not interpret them as current simulations.
