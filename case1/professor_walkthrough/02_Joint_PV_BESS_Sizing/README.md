# Joint equipment sizing: PV, BESS, inverter and cost

The PyPSA/Linopy campaign evaluated 6,840 discrete MILP scenario tasks over a fixed synthetic 2025 demand realization. Each task co-optimized battery operation along with technology choices, using the full year of information. The economic results are planning benchmarks under perfect foresight, not evidence of an implementable real-time controller or an as-built LV network.

## Why the next stage followed

Technology size and annual self-sufficiency were the first answers. They did not yet establish whether the connected houses received acceptable voltage.
