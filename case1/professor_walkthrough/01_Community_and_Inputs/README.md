# Why El Arenal, and what data can the models rely on?

We began with a real location and an incomplete utility picture. The study used NASA POWER 2025 irradiance and 35 mapped properties, then matched EPM transformer identifiers and mapped LV conductor nodes. The GIS graph offers a stronger backbone than guessed straight-line feeders, but the property service drops and customer assignments remain modelled assumptions.
