# Why El Arenal, and what data can the models rely on?

We began with a real location and an incomplete utility picture. The study used NASA POWER 2025 irradiance and 35 mapped properties. An initial provisional connection sketch helped establish the candidate territories; later work recovered EPM transformer identifiers and GIS conductor nodes. The GIS graph offers a stronger backbone than guessed straight-line feeders, but the property service drops and customer assignments remain modelled assumptions.
