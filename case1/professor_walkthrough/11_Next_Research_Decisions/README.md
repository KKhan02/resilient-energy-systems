# Next research decisions and evidence gates

The seven-house, 94022 case now demonstrates **annual SOC-consistent active-power feedback** and revalidated AC operation. The 12-house, 106039 manufacturer-SOC baseline also passes its reference full-year 0.95–1.05 pu magnitude screen. Neither result makes every archived hardware configuration feasible or field-certified.

## Work still needed

1. **Finish the entire eligible fixed-hardware campaign:** manufacturer SOC, independent full-year reference AC and nine full-year PF/service-length cases per candidate. Retain failed/infeasible solutions and complete the energy-cost versus feeder-feasibility frontier. Never repeat the already archived 6,840 investment tasks.
2. **Generalize feedback:** when full-year AC fails, derive corrective P/Q constraints without breaking annual SOC chronology, efficiency, no unserved load and actual battery/inverter hardware capability. Repeat complete annual AC validation.
3. **Equipment verification:** confirm transformer nameplates, LV phases, actual service conductors and impedances, household-to-transformer assignments, battery current ratings and inverter nighttime reactive-power capabilities. Current EPM GIS results are conditional.
4. **Loss-aware economics:** integrate feeder losses and relevant inverter conversion/standby losses with the energy model. Review source-based installation/BOS and battery-power costs.
5. **Uncertainty and operation:** vary demand/solar realizations and compare perfect-foresight dispatch to forecast-based control, especially in stressed low-PV periods.
6. **Community resilience and adaptability:** quantify outage survival separately from normal-operation self-sufficiency; later test additional households, physical reassignment and hosting capacity without inventing network connections.

The site-specific 0.95–1.05 pu band is a proposed **research engineering target**. The earlier 0.97–1.03 pu band is a strict research scenario, not an EPM regulatory criterion.
