# Coordinated Q control with thermal and voltage constraints

The coordinated AC search evaluates positive and negative Q candidates against minimum voltage, maximum voltage, transformer loading and LV line loading. We then swept Q capability rather than relying only on 0.25 and 0.48. For transformer 106039, all 40 selected critical hours passed from Q ratio 0.20 at the midpoint or 0.25 at the head in the reference conditions. Transformer 94022 retained unresolved violations.
