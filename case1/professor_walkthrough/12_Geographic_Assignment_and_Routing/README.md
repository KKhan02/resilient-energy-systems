# Stage 12. Geographic assignment and routing

**Research question:** Does a reasonable geographic connection and routing rule produce an electrically acceptable community network?

The earlier Study A results used provisional household cohorts. EPM's mapped LV line geometry then provided a more defensible spatial reference, but the household service connections, conductor details and transformer attribution still require field verification.

1. **[A0 versus A1, mapped LV territories](01_A0_A1_mapped_LV_territories.png).** Compare the original provisional assignment with the geographic-nearest assignment. Both retain the same 34-household five-territory population; changing assignment is not a change in annual household demand. Show the mapped LV backbone separately from assumed service drops.
2. **[Transformer 92395, proposed STAR versus MST](02_92395_STAR_MST_annual_screen.png).** The short proposed MST route does not automatically satisfy household-voltage limits. Existing annual screening finds 36/36 STAR scenarios passing, while 24/36 proposed MST scenarios fail. The chart is from a *historical pre-correction study* and does not represent a physically verified construction route.

**Why Stage 13 follows:** A favourable geography and an optimized hourly energy schedule cannot establish nodal AC feasibility. We therefore replay voltage and thermal constraints through the year, then document corrective dispatch and network assumptions.
