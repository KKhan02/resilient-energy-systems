Rule: each house's fair share of solar is G ÷ 4. Houses consuming at or below that share pay nothing and receive nothing. Houses above it collectively cover the net cost of the grid exchange — (imported × purchase price) minus (exported × delivery price) — split either by how much each one exceeds its share, or by fixed coefficients you set for the above-average houses. If exports are worth more than what was imported, that net cost is zero or negative and nobody is billed.
