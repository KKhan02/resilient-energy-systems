# Data provenance and uncertainties

## User-supplied check-in PDF
"17 Nov_First Checkin.pdf" pp. 1-7: study regions and survey overview. Page 6 has the number of respondents PER community, not the total number of households living there. Page 7 supplies a coordinate per COMMUNITY, not house GPS points. Pages 8-10 are screenshots of questionnaire spreadsheet records; there is no accessible raw workbook. Do not publish personally identifying interview fields.

reference/community_catalog_checkin.csv transcribes source coordinates and community counts. Check place-name spelling and coordinates. Example community grid statuses on p. 4 are coded ONLY where explicitly named, not as a survey-wide assignment. Verify current electrification status independently.

## Missing layers
- Recent georeferenced building footprints/house positions and total houses/households.
- Community boundary, grid, island geography, feasible routes, soil/land/water restrictions.
- Permission-cleared underlying survey and codebook, appliance ownership, usage, productive services and affordability.
- Reliability targets, connection tariffs, CAPEX/OPEX, irradiation, equipment and network design parameters.

## Separation from Colombia research
SEEDExchange already has shared PV/BESS PyPSA studies, pandapower and clustered LV designs. Reusing verified code structures is permitted after review, but NEVER inherit Colombian weather, geographic coordinates, line models, loads, tariff assumptions, network names or cost results as Ghana evidence.
